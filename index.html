Speak With Area71 ↗
Is Launchpad71 only a training course?

No. The Launchpad71 brochure combines done-for-you launch services with hands-on learning, from product research and sourcing through listing, shipment and PPC.

Will Area71 help me learn to manage the business?

Yes. Practical learning is a central program component, intended to help you understand the work and eventually run your business with greater independence.

What is the duration of Launchpad71?

The brochure states a one-year project duration. The start date, support commitments and milestones should be confirmed in your signed agreement.

How long will Area71 support the first inventory?

The brochure describes support until the initial inventory is completely sold. Clarify how this commitment operates within the one-year project duration and any contractual limitations.

Do I need prior Amazon FBA experience?

The program is presented as guided learning, but you should still discuss your starting knowledge, time availability and goals with the Area71 team.

What investment do I need?

Product, manufacturing, shipping, Amazon and advertising costs depend on the selected business plan. Ask for a clear individualized estimate, including the Launchpad71 service fee.

Are specific sales or profits guaranteed?

No specific profits or investment returns are promised on this website. Actual results depend on demand, competition, product economics and execution.
